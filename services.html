Our services

Non-medical assistance for daily living, connection, and a safer home.

Creativa Care specializes exclusively in non-medical support designed to help with activities of daily living, foster mental stimulation, and keep a clean, safe home environment.

A. Personal care at home

We assist with activities of daily living while strictly respecting privacy and personal boundaries.

  • Hygiene & bathing: full-body bathing, shower assistance, and hair and skin care
  • Dressing & grooming: help choosing clothing, dressing, and daily grooming
  • Toileting & incontinence care: discreet, respectful bathroom and hygiene support
  • Mobility & transfers: safe bed-to-chair transfers, mobility aids, and walking support to help prevent falls

Privacy first. Personal care is always offered with consent, patience, and respect for the client’s comfort and dignity.

B. Companionship & social engagement

Social isolation can affect mental and physical health. Our caregivers provide meaningful human connection.

Cognitive & recreational activities

Board games, cards, and puzzles to keep the mind engaged.

Entertainment

Watching television, reading, or listening to music together.

Outdoor activity

Accompanied walks, fresh-air strolls, and light outdoor engagement.

Physical exercises

Assistance with prescribed gentle stretching, range-of-motion exercises, or routine activity encouraged by family or healthcare providers. Caregivers do not provide medical treatment.

C. Light household assistance

A clean, orderly home supports safety and peace of mind. Our team handles routine household chores, including:

  • Laundry: washing, drying, folding, and putting clothes away
  • Floor care: sweeping and mopping
  • Kitchen maintenance: washing dishes, wiping counters, and keeping the kitchen tidy
  • Bed & linen management: making beds and changing linens regularly
  • Wardrobe organization: organizing closets and arranging clothes neatly

Care packages

Support can be arranged hourly or on a regular schedule, based on what the household needs. Call us to discuss a plan that fits your family.
