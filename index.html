Why families call us

Support that helps people remain at home

Many seniors and people with temporary or ongoing limitations prefer to age in place rather than move into a facility before they are ready. Creativa Care offers scheduled, person-centred support so daily life stays safer and more comfortable.

  • Respite for family caregivers, to help prevent burnout
  • Hourly or scheduled care tailored to each household
  • Local caregivers serving Kingston and nearby communities
